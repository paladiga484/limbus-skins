# limbus-skins 1.9.0

Two new boss fights, new identities, and everything moves like Limbus
- **Nothing There** (EXP Luxcavation, its own tab), as Lobotomy Corporation animates it: its first
  form, then its last, saying "Hello?" and "I love you?" as it attacks.
- **WhiteNight** (its own tab): its apostles, then WhiteNight among them, in their Lobotomy
  Corporation animations.
- **Twilight:** every bird has a kit of its own; the Apocalypse Bird is a real boss now.
- **New identities:** Vespa, Moses, Ezra, Don Quixote (de la Mancha) and Jia Mu.
- **All 12 sinners can fight at once** (`all-sinners`, on by default; Boss Raids keep their limit).
- **The Ruina IDs move like Limbus,** and are bigger. Roland fights with his workshop weapons.
- **Cards in colour:** every identity of ours has its own card and profile picture, and its name
  in its colour.
- **Faster launches:** nothing is rebuilt when nothing changed (it used to rebuild every time).
